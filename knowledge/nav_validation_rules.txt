NAV validation ensures that the Net Asset Value of a fund reflects accurate valuation of securities and liabilities.

Key NAV validation checks include:

[R1] Security prices must be sourced from approved pricing vendors.
[R2] Price variance above tolerance levels must trigger investigation. A day-over-day price move beyond the agreed tolerance is treated as a price variance break.
[R3] Income accruals must align with coupon schedules and dividend declarations. Recorded income exceeding expected income indicates an income accrual mismatch.
[R4] Expense accruals must follow management agreements and contractual fee rates. Recorded expense exceeding the expected fee is an expense accrual variance.
[R5] Distribution accruals should not exceed available income. A recorded distribution above the expected distribution is a distribution accrual discrepancy.
[R6] Position quantities must reconcile with custody records. Differences between accounting quantities and custody records are a position quantity mismatch.
